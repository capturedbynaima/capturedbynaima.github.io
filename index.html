Hi, I’m Naima.

I am a photographer and visual storyteller based in Kano. I started taking photographs in my secondary school days, and my love for it has only grown since.

There is an art to photography that I find truly beautiful: capturing a moment and turning it into something that can be looked back on, remembered and felt again.
