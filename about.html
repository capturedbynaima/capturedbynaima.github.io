Hi, I’m Naima.

I am a photographer and visual storyteller based in Kano, Nigeria. I love capturing people, emotions and moments.

I started taking photographs in my secondary school days, and my interest has only grown since. There is an art to photography that I find truly beautiful: the idea of capturing a moment and turning it into something that can be looked back on, remembered and felt again.

I am drawn to rich color, natural expression, and the small moments between the big ones. I enjoy creating images that feel natural, expressive, and true to the moment.

Through my photography, I hope to capture moments that tell a story and create memories that last.
